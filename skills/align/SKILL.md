# Align

My words are a pointer, not a spec. Read the relevant files (and any screenshots) first, then restate what I asked as you now understand it: open with one line — my whole ask in aggregate — then one item per line, in the repo's real names and actual code state. Cite `path:line`. Process instructions (delegate, commit, don't edit yet) are items too.

Label what you filled in that I didn't say. If a phrase has multiple readings, list them — don't pick ("more compact": tighter spacing or fewer rows?).

Then stop — no edits — so we confirm we're aligned first. Once I confirm, the restatement plus my corrections is the spec, not my original wording.
